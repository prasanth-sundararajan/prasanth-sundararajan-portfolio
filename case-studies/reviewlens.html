ReviewLens: a week of reviews, read in five minutes

A LangChain pipeline that reads every customer review, in English, Tamil or Tanglish, tags and scores it, and writes a weekly report a manager can act on in about five minutes.

01 · Challenge

The problem worth solving

A restaurant chain or online store gets hundreds of reviews every week from Google, Zomato, Swiggy and Amazon, in English, Tamil and Tanglish. Read by hand, urgent complaints such as food safety issues, wrong orders or rude staff can sit unseen for days.

Worse, nobody can say with numbers what customers complain about most. An LLM can read the reviews, but free-text answers are hard to count, and a model that also does the arithmetic will sooner or later report numbers that do not match the data.

02 · Approach

How I built it

  • Structured output for every review. A ChatPromptTemplate | llm.with_structured_output(ReviewAnalysis) chain returns a Pydantic model: sentiment, category, urgency, language, a one-line summary and a suggested reply. Fields are Literal enums, so invalid values are rejected.
  • Retry, then flag. Invalid outputs are retried twice. Anything still failing is marked needs_review instead of crashing the run.
  • Numbers from pandas, never from the LLM. Sentiment split, categories, urgency and platforms are counted in code, so the report always matches the table.
  • Map → reduce report. Per-review summaries are grouped by category and condensed in chunks, then one chain writes the top complaints, top praise, the urgent count and three action items.
  • Batched and swappable. chain.batch(..., max_concurrency=8) processes a typical week of about 250 reviews in roughly a minute, and the provider (OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic or an offline mock) is one environment variable.

04 · Key features

What it does

Upload & validation

Empty and duplicate rows are dropped before any LLM call, and a review limit protects the API budget.

HIGH-urgency filter

The reviews that need a reply today, such as health, refund, rude staff or wrong order, in one list.

Weekly report

Top complaints, top praise, the urgent count and three concrete action items.

Trend vs last week

Save a week and the next report shows ▲▼ movement automatically.

Replies in the customer's language

Suggested replies in Tamil for Tamil reviews and in Tanglish for Tanglish ones.

Monday email + CSV

The saved report and an Excel-ready enriched CSV are emailed every Monday.

Guardrails & safety

Built to be safe in production

Live, tested protections in the deployed app, not just a demo.

Input limits

CSV only, up to 5 MB and 500 reviews per run. Control characters are stripped and very long reviews are truncated.

Prompt-injection defence

Reviews are treated strictly as data. Injection phrases are neutralised and flagged, so a review can't instruct the model.

Validated output

Labels and scores are checked against a schema, invalid answers are retried and then marked for review, and HTML is never rendered raw.

Cost & abuse caps

Token and timeout limits per call, 5 analyses per hour per visitor and a daily cap for the whole app.

Safe handling

Harmful content is skipped before reaching the model, and API keys are masked in messages and logs.

06 · Result

What it delivers

A manager reads one page instead of hundreds of reviews and knows what to fix this week. Urgent reviews surface with a ready-to-edit reply, and every number in the report comes from code.

The app has 15 offline tests that run without an API key, and it is live behind HTTPS with automatic certificates.
