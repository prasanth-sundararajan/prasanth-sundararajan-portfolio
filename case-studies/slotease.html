SlotEase: appointment booking that cannot double-book

A multi-business booking platform for salons, clinics and local services. Customers pick a slot, pay a small deposit through Dodo Payments, and the booking lands in the staff member's Google Calendar. I designed, built, tested and deployed it end to end.

01 · Challenge

The problem worth solving

Salons and clinics lose money to no-shows, double-bookings and the endless “is 4 pm free?” messages. A booking tool that fixes this has to get several hard things right at the same time.

Two customers must never get the same staff member at the same time, even when they click in the same second. A slot should be held while the customer pays, but not forever. Every open browser should see a slot disappear the moment it is taken. And a browser redirect that says “paid” is not proof of payment, so the booking must only be confirmed by the payment provider itself.

02 · Approach

How I built it

  • The database is the final referee. A PostgreSQL exclusion constraint on (staff_id, tstzrange(start_at, end_at)) for active bookings makes an overlap impossible, whatever the application code does. A violation becomes a clean “slot taken” response, and the service tries the next free staff member.
  • A 10-minute hold, not a lock. A new booking is inserted as pending_payment with hold_expires_at. It occupies the slot while the customer pays. A scheduler job runs every minute, expires lapsed holds and frees the slot.
  • Payments are confirmed by the webhook only. Dodo Payments webhooks are verified with the Standard Webhooks signature and processed idempotently by event id. A late payment for an expired hold is re-confirmed if the slot is still free, and refunded automatically if it is not.
  • Real-time over WebSockets. A public slot channel pushes slot.taken and slot.freed to every open booking page. The owner dashboard channel uses a short-lived, single-use ticket so long-lived tokens never appear in URLs.
  • Two-way Google Calendar sync. Confirmed bookings are pushed to the staff member's calendar, and their busy times are pulled back so personal events block slots too.
  • Business rules per business. Each business sets its deposit percentage (15% by default) and a cancellation cutoff that decides whether a cancellation is refunded.

04 · Key features

What it does

Public booking page

Each business gets its own page: pick a service, a staff member (or anyone free) and a time.

Staff schedules

Working hours, time off and buffers per staff member feed one slot engine.

Deposits and refunds

Deposits through Dodo Payments hosted checkout; refunds follow the business's own cutoff rule.

Live owner dashboard

New, confirmed, cancelled and rescheduled bookings appear instantly over WebSockets.

Google Calendar sync

Bookings go to the staff calendar; busy calendar events block slots.

Secure auth

Google sign-in and JWT access tokens with refresh tokens, plus role checks for owners and staff.

Guardrails & safety

Built to be safe in production

Live, tested protections in the deployed app, not just a demo.

Payments you can trust

Bookings are confirmed only by a signature-verified, idempotent Dodo webhook, never by the browser. Refunds are recorded before they're sent and retried automatically.

No double-booking

A PostgreSQL exclusion constraint makes overlapping bookings impossible, even under concurrent requests. Unpaid holds expire after 10 minutes.

Abuse limits

Rate limits on login, sign-up, booking and contact, shared across workers through Redis, plus request size caps.

Tenant isolation

Every owner and staff query is scoped to their own business, with tests proving one business can't read another's data.

Hardened delivery

Strict Content-Security-Policy, HSTS, single-use WebSocket tickets, encrypted Google tokens and CSRF-protected OAuth.

06 · Result

What it delivers

A slot vanishes from other open browsers as soon as it is booked, and the database itself refuses an overlapping booking. Payments are confirmed only by verified webhooks, late payments are reconciled automatically, and refunds follow each business's cutoff rule.

The project ships with 221 automated tests (151 backend and 70 frontend) and 85% backend test coverage, including payments, refunds and concurrency. It runs live behind HTTPS on a self-managed VPS.
